01. About Me

Hello! I'm an Electrical Engineering student currently in my 7th Semester at COMSATS University Islamabad. I operate at the intersection of low-level hardware design, embedded firmware development, and modern software design.

Before joining COMSATS University Islamabad, I completed my schooling and college-level Pre-Engineering education at Army Public School & College (APS & CS), Bagh, AJK . This academic background helped build my foundation in Mathematics, Physics, and Electrical Engineering.

My core engineering focus is on Battery Management Systems (BMS), power electronics, and embedded microcontrollers (STM32, ESP32, Arduino). I thrive on turning complex hardware challenges into real-world, functional prototypes.

Beyond core electrical engineering, I possess strong software versatility—proficient in C/C++, Python, Java, MATLAB—and creative skillsets in Graphic Design & Web Development, allowing me to build complete end-to-end hardware-to-software solutions.
